Classroom controls

Every deck uses the same engine. Arrow keys, Space, or click the right side of the slide to advance. Click the left side to go back. O opens a slide grid, F toggles fullscreen, S opens presenter view with notes and a timer, ? shows the shortcut list. Print the page to PDF — one slide per landscape page. Deep links use #slide-3; a reload keeps your place. There is no wraparound from the last slide back to the first.
