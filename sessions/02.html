Revenue streams

How digital firms actually get paid

Commission

Take-rate

A cut of each closed order. The classic marketplace model.

Advertising

Sell attention

Seller ads and brand ads. Often the profit engine even when take-rate is thin.

Subscription

Rent the access

Monthly fee. Deferred revenue until you deliver the period.

Payment fee

Move the money

MDR, VA fees, disbursement. Fintech P&L inside a super-app.

Logistics

Move the box

Delivery mark-up or 3PL. Returns are a cost centre in disguise.

Hybrid

Stack them

Gojek-style: commissions + ads + payments + subscriptions.

Seller economics

GMV is not take-home

A Rp 100,000 paid order can become a much smaller bank credit after commission, programme fees, ads, and shipping you subsidise.

Fee schedules change by category and month. Shopee and Tokopedia now publish layered admin / commission ranges — do not memorise a single “2–5%.” Look up the current seller centre before you advise a client.

Teaching waterfall

  • Customer pays 100
  • − platform commission / admin
  • − optional programme / free-shipping share
  • − seller-funded discount
  • − ads attributed to the SKU
  • = cash you might see, later
As of 2026 mid-year reporting, Shopee admin often sits in a wide percent band by subcategory plus a small per-order fee; Tokopedia uses platform + dynamic commission bands. Quote ranges, not a fake precision. Always: check seller centre.

PSAK 72 · IFRS 15

Gross or net: principal vs agent

Principal · gross

You control the goods before they go to the customer. Revenue is the amount the customer pays (subject to discounts). COGS and fees are separate.

Agent · net

You arrange for someone else to provide the good or service. Revenue is the commission or fee you keep.

A marketplace is often an agent. The brand selling from its own warehouse is usually a principal. A super-app can be both on the same invoice — read the contract.
Indicators of control: inventory risk, ability to set price, primary responsibility for fulfilment. Do not turn this into a full PSAK 72 lecture; give them the test and one example each.

Tax, lightly

Marketplaces and the tax file

PPN

If the seller is PKP, sales of taxable goods still need a valid faktur pajak via Coretax / e-Faktur — a platform payout is not a tax invoice.

PPh

Marketplace withholding, if any, is a credit — not “tax finished.” Check the current DJP / platform notice; rules have moved.

UMKM final

Some small sellers use PPh final UMKM. Digital GMV still needs a record that matches the bank.

Coretax is DJP’s core administration system; e-Faktur is used as a module inside it for many PKP. Confirm the live process before filing advice.

Do not pretend to be their tax advisor. Point them at Coretax and the need for records. Session 09 returns to UMKM tax rails.

Other models in one pass

Subscription, freemium, social commerce

Subscription

Cash first, revenue later. Unearned revenue until the month is delivered. Churn is a revenue-quality metric.

Freemium

The free tier is a CAC engine. Conversion rate to paid is the P&L, not sign-ups.

Social commerce

TikTok Shop / Live: demand and checkout collapse into one video. Returns and ‘impulse COGS’ spike.

One minute each. Social commerce is why Tokopedia × TikTok mattered.

Studio

Audit the platform story

15 minutes · groups of 4
