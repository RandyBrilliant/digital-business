Brief

Build a plan someone could start

A comprehensive digital business plan for a real local SME or a startup concept grounded in Medan (or your hometown). It must include:

  • Market and competitor view a sceptic believes
  • Complete Business Model Canvas
  • 30-day marketing calendar and a channel budget
  • Payment and settlement design
  • Customer journey with CRM artefacts
  • KPI dashboard with sources
  • 90-day timeline, budget, and unit economics
  • 15-minute presentation + Q&A
Real SME preferred. Fiction allowed if the operations feel walkable.

Section 7 is new vs the old template. That is the accounting programme talking.

Financials bar

If this is missing, the plan is not finished

Unit

Price, COGS, MDR / commission, ads per order, contribution.

Month

Volume that is not a hockey stick without a channel plan.

Cash

Settlements lag. Stock is often cash. Owner drawings exist.

GMV is allowed as a footnote. Revenue follows PSAK 72. If you cannot defend principal vs agent, we will ask in Q&A.
This is the slide that changes their weekend.

Old rubric: 15 market, 20 model, 20 marketing, 15 tech, 15 financials, 15 presentation. We shifted weight toward financials + books/tax.

What loses marks fast

Please do not

  • Cite Tokopedia as a GoTo marketplace
  • Plan a Bukalapak physical-goods shop
  • Use “13% SMEs digital” or “30M Mitra”
  • Call GMV revenue
  • Forget MDR and commission
  • Present a canvas with empty cost/revenue
  • Promise virality as the channel strategy
  • Skip journals / COA because ‘we are marketing’
  • Read 20 slides in 15 minutes
  • Say “code review” with a straight face
Humour on the last bullet. They know.

Run of show

How the room will work

Order

Posted the day before. Be in the room at minute −5. Laptop tested offline — classroom Wi-Fi is a rumour.

Timer

A visible countdown. At 15:00 we move to questions. At 20:00 we change teams.

Files

PDF of slides + the report. Relative links, local images. No live-only Figma.

If you are presenting today, skip assignment slide later and go to congratulations after the last team.

Peer sheet

Listen like an accountant

While others present, tick four boxes on a scrap page:

  • Did they name principal vs agent?
  • Did contribution appear, not only GMV?
  • Is there a settlement / MDR story?
  • Would you put your family’s money in this for 90 days?
Keeps the audience awake. Collect sheets if you want participation marks.

Q&A they should expect

We will ask some version of these

Money

  • Show contribution on one order
  • When does cash actually arrive?
  • What if volume is half?

Control

  • Who can withdraw?
  • Where does the faktur live?
  • What assumption do you kill first?
Read two questions aloud. Then studio or presentations.

Studio · if this is briefing week

Red-team your outline

15 minutes · pairs

Swap one-page outlines. The partner may only ask: Where is cash? Where is fee? Where is tax? What assumption dies first? Rewrite the exec summary in five lines after the bruising.
