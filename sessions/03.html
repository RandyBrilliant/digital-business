Why this session

Marketing is a ledger problem

You will not become media buyers today. You will learn to read the numbers that decide whether a campaign is an investment or a leak.

Spend

Where the cash went

Ads, influencers, marketplace boost, agency retainers — all of it hits beban pemasaran.

Return

What came back

Orders, repeat visits, contribution margin. Revenue without margin is a vanity win.

Control

What you can audit

Invoices vs platform dashboards, prepaid ad credit, and fees withheld before settlement.

CAC CLV ROAS beban pemasaran margin kontribusi
Accounting hook. Students who think this is a ‘business elective’ need this slide. Marketing spend is often the messiest opex line in an UMKM file. Preview that we will journal ads and marketplace fees later.

Definition

What digital marketing is

All marketing that uses digital channels to find, convert, and keep customers — and that can be measured click by click.

01 · Multi-channel

An ecosystem, not a post

SEO, search ads, Instagram, WhatsApp, email, and content only work as a system.

02 · Measurable

Every click can be a number

Impressions, clicks, conversion, cost. If you cannot tie spend to an outcome, you cannot close the books honestly.

03 · Two-way

Customers talk back

Reviews, DMs, and comments are both marketing and customer-service risk.

It is not “having a website.” It is a repeatable way to buy attention, earn trust, and turn both into cash.
Ask for a one-line definition from the room before revealing the three cards. Push back on ‘social media = digital marketing’. Mention email and search still convert many local businesses.

Digital does not replace traditional. A radio spot still works for a Medan audience that commutes. The best mix is the one your customer actually sees — and that you can afford.
Old deck used a fake street address as the traditional example; we dropped it. Use ‘radio in Medan morning drive’ vs ‘Instagram geo-ads’. Ask: which is easier to audit? Digital leaves a trail — if someone exports it.

Running example

Meet Kopi Nusantara

A fictional independent café in Medan. Two baristas, one owner who also keeps the books. We will take this shop through every funnel stage.

Rp 28k

Average ticket (AOV)

62%

Gross margin on drinks

Rp 2.4jt

Monthly marketing budget

Medan

Campus + office catchment

The owner’s question is not “are we famous?” It is “does Rp 2.4 million of ads and promotions leave us more contribution margin than doing nothing?”
Numbers are teaching fiction, labelled as such if anyone asks. AOV 28k is plausible for a decent cup + snack. 62% drink margin is generous but useful. Budget 2.4jt is small on purpose — UMKM scale.

01 · Awareness

“I just discovered this brand”

Goal

Maximum relevant visibility

Not fame. The right people in Medan should see the café exist — students, office workers, weekend families.

Kopi Nusantara

  • Instagram Reels of latte art and the morning rush
  • A food blogger visit, paid as an invoice (not “free coffee”)
  • Google Ads on “coffee shop Medan” and “café near campus”

Accountant watch

Awareness spend is usually period expense. A prepaid Ads credit is an asset until it is served. A blogger invoice needs a name, NPWP if required, and a clear deliverable — otherwise it is a fuzzy entertainment cost.

impresi faktur / invoice beban periode
Pause on the blogger: students often treat ‘free product for a post’ as zero cost. It is barter. Record the COGS and the marketing expense. Mention NPWP / bukti potong if the fee is material.

02 · Consideration

“I am comparing my options”

What the customer does

  • Reads Google reviews and looks at photos
  • Checks the menu and price on Instagram or the website
  • Asks a friend in the group chat
  • Compares with the café two streets over

Kopi Nusantara moves

  • A simple site: single-origin beans, 4.8★ reviews, opening hours
  • Retargeting ads to people who visited the site but did not come
  • Reply to every review in 24 hours — service is marketing
Consideration is where trust is bought cheaply: honest photos, reply speed, and a price that matches the room. Fancy ads cannot repair a 3.2★ rating.
Ask: if two cafés cost the same, what do you check? Reviews. Then: who owns the review risk? Operations. Marketing cannot outspend a dirty cup.

03 · Purchase

“I am ready to buy”

Remove friction

Make paying easy

WhatsApp ordering, a QR menu, QRIS on the counter. If the queue is the checkout, you lose the convert.

Give a reason

A clear offer

First-visit “buy 1 get 1” on a weekday afternoon — time-boxed so it does not wreck weekend margin.

On the books

Promo is not free

BOGO is a sales discount or extra COGS, not a marketing miracle. Decide the account and stick to it.

QRIS diskon penjualan harga pokok WhatsApp Business
Accounting argument: treat BOGO consistently. Either net against sales or record full sales + promo COGS. Inconsistency breaks contribution margin. QRIS is the expected rail, not a legal mandate for every warung — we correct that myth in Session 06.

04 · Retention

“I keep coming back”

Kopi Nusantara

  • Stamp card: buy 9, get 1 free
  • WhatsApp broadcast for Tuesday brew specials
  • Birthday pour, logged in a simple customer sheet
Acquisition is usually more expensive than retention — often cited in a wide band of about 5–25× depending on the industry. Do not treat “5×” as a law. Compute your CAC vs the cost of a stamp-card freebie.

Loyalty on the ledger

Unredeemed stamps are a small deferred obligation if material. For a café, they are often immaterial — but the habit of tracking redemptions is the point. A free drink is COGS, not “brand love.”

program loyalitas kewajiban tangguhan CLV
Correct the old deck’s flat ‘5× cheaper’. HBR 2014 summarises the range as 5–25× by study and industry (Gallo, citing acquisition-vs-retention research). Directionally true, precisely messy. Students should calculate, not quote.

05 · Advocacy

“I tell my friends about it”

Kopi Nusantara

  • Referral: invite a friend, both get 20% off once
  • #KopiNusantara on a Saturday pour — opt-in, not forced
  • Review reward: a stamp, not cash, for an honest Google review

Why accountants care

Word of mouth has no media invoice — and a very real cost if a referral discount is untracked. Paid reviews without disclosure are both an ethics problem and a tax-documentation problem.

Advocacy is the cheapest acquisition channel you cannot buy directly. You can only earn it — then measure referral codes so the discount does not vanish into “miscellaneous.”
Short slide. Ask who last referred a café. Then: would you trust a 5-star review that says ‘got a free drink for this’? Disclosure matters. Move on to channels.

SEO · Search engine optimization

Be findable when intent is hot

On-page

  • Title and heading that match the search
  • Menu, hours, address, photos
  • Pages a human can actually read

Off-page

  • Google Business Profile
  • Citations: consistent name and area
  • Reviews you reply to

Technical

  • Fast, mobile page
  • HTTPS, readable on a cheap phone
  • No mystery “under construction”
SEO is a slow asset. The work is mostly labour this month; the traffic can arrive for years. Accountants: that is still usually an expense, not a capitalised “brand asset,” unless a standard specifically allows it.
Local SEO is the win for a café. Google Business Profile is free and underused. Do not promise ‘rank #1’. Promise ‘be complete and consistent’. IAS 38 / PSAK 19 generally keep internally generated brands off the balance sheet — mention lightly.

SEM · Search engine marketing

Pay per click, then demand a return

How a Google Ad is built

  • Campaign — objective (visits, calls, purchases)
  • Ad group — one tight theme, e.g. “café Medan”
  • Keywords + ad — the query and the promise
  • Landing — hours, map, WhatsApp. Not a dead homepage

Beginner budget discipline

  • Start small and cap the daily limit
  • Run at least a few days before you judge
  • Test 3–5 creatives, not one masterpiece
  • Judge on ROAS and contribution, not clicks

ROAS = Revenue attributed to ads ÷ Ad spend

Auction and Quality Score in one sentence: you do not always win by bidding more. Then: ROAS of 4 looks great until contribution margin is 30% and delivery eats the rest. We compute that on the worked-example slide.

Content marketing

Teach the thing you sell

Kopi Nusantara content

  • Blog or carousel: “How to choose beans for rumah”
  • Reel: 20-second pour-over, no hard sell
  • Owner story: why the blend is named after a harvest
The 80 / 20 habit: most posts help or entertain. A minority sell. A feed that is only discount codes trains people to wait.

On the books

A freelancer invoice for a video is marketing expense. Staff time is payroll, not “free content.” If you reuse a video for six months, it is still usually expensed — resist capitalising a Reel.

konten edukasi jasa freelancer beban gaji
Content is how SEO gets something to rank. Connect back: a ‘how to choose beans’ page can rank and also support consideration. Staff time is the hidden cost UMKM owners forget.

The rest of the mix

Instagram, WhatsApp, email, influencers

Instagram

Best for awareness and the look of the room. Shopping tags help; they do not replace a WhatsApp close. Treat boosts as SEM with prettier pictures.

WhatsApp Business

Catalogue, labels, quick replies. This is operations plus retention. Broadcast lists without consent burn trust — and can violate platform rules.

Email

Owned channel. A weekly note beats a daily blast. Track unsubscribe as a health metric, not a nuisance.

Influencers

Buy an audience you cannot reach yet. Contract the deliverable, usage rights, and disclosure. Pay against invoice, not to a personal e-wallet with no name.

Influencer payment hygiene is the accounting gold. ‘Transfer to my GoPay’ with no invoice is how files fail an audit. One minute, then metrics.

Metrics an accountant reads

Five numbers that earn a seat

MetricFormulaWhy it matters
CACSales + marketing cost ÷ new customersWhat you paid to buy a first visit
CLVAOV × purchases per year × years × marginWhether that CAC was sane
ROASAttributed revenue ÷ ad spendCampaign-level efficiency
Conversion rateBuyers ÷ visitors (or clicks)Where the funnel leaks
Spend / revenueMarketing cost ÷ salesBudget test vs last month

Rule of thumb, not a law: many digital businesses aim for CLV well above CAC (often talked about as ~3×). Compute it on contribution margin, not on gross sales.

Walk CAC and CLV slowly. The 3× CLV:CAC ratio is a common practitioner heuristic, not an IFRS rule. Stress contribution margin. ROAS ignores COGS — that is why the next two slides exist.

Studio

Plan a funnel + budget

18 minutes · groups of 3–4

Your brief

Pick a real Medan business you can describe in one sentence — bika bakery, laundry, tutoring, barbershop, or a warung. Budget: Rp 2,000,000 for 30 days.

  • One action per funnel stage
  • Split the Rp 2jt across channels
  • Estimate CAC and a break-even ROAS

Present in 90 seconds

  • Who is the customer, really?
  • Where does the money go?
  • What will you measure on day 30?
  • What hits the books as expense vs discount?
Start a visible timer. Walk the room. Ban ‘we will go viral’. Force a channel split that adds to 2jt. After 18 minutes, hear 3 groups max or you will blow the close. If the class is huge, pair groups.

Recap

Four things to keep

01

Funnel, not vibes

Awareness is wide. Advocacy is earned. Name the leak before you buy more ads.

02

SEO, SEM, content

Earn the click, buy the click, give the click something worth reading.

03

ROAS is not profit

Subtract COGS, fees, and the promo. Contribution margin is the adult metric.

04

Every campaign is a journal

Prepaid ads, influencer invoices, withheld fees. If it cannot be reconciled, it cannot be managed.

Four beats, one breath each. Then assignment. Do not open a new topic.
