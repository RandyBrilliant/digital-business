You will not become media buyers today. You will learn to read whether a campaign is an investment or a leak. Ads, influencers, marketplace boost, retainers — all hit beban pemasaran. WhatsApp ordering, QR menu, QRIS on the counter. If the queue is the checkout, you lose the convert. First-visit buy-1-get-1 on a weekday afternoon — time-boxed so it does not wreck weekend margin. Treat BOGO consistently: net against sales or full sales + promo COGS. QRIS is the expected rail, not a legal mandate for every warung — Session 06.

Acquisition is usually more expensive than retention — HBR 2014 (Gallo) summarises a 5–25× range by study and industry. Do not treat “5×” as a law. Compute your CAC vs the cost of a stamp-card freebie. Unredeemed stamps are a small deferred obligation if material. A free drink is COGS, not brand love.

Instagram: awareness and the look of the room. Shopping tags help; they do not replace a WhatsApp close. Treat boosts as SEM with prettier pictures. WhatsApp Business: catalogue, labels, quick replies — operations plus retention. Broadcast without consent burns trust. Email: weekly beats daily; unsubscribe is a health metric. Five numbers

CAC

Cost ÷ new customers

CLV

AOV × freq × years × margin

ROAS

Revenue ÷ ad spend

CR

Buyers ÷ visitors

S/R

Marketing ÷ sales

Walk CAC and CLV slowly. Many digital businesses aim for CLV well above CAC (often talked about as ~3×) — a practitioner heuristic, not an IFRS rule. Compute on contribution margin, not gross sales. ROAS ignores COGS — that is why the next two slides exist. Conversion rate shows where the funnel leaks. Spend/revenue is the budget test vs last month.

Top up Google/Meta: Dr Prepaid advertising, Cr Cash. Ads served: Dr Marketing expense, Cr Prepaid. Influencer invoice: Dr Marketing, Cr Cash or AP. Marketplace commission withheld: Dr Selling/platform fee, Cr Marketplace receivable. Settlement arrives net: Dr Bank, Cr receivable. Reconcile gross order, fee, net cash — or revenue is understated and fees disappear. Tease PSAK 72 gross vs net. Do not quote a single 2–5% fee as fact.

Did the ads work?

Kopi Nusantara, one month, teaching figures: sales 48jt; variable COGS 38% = 18.24jt; ads IG+Google 1.8jt; influencer 0.6jt; 80 new customers; campaign-tagged revenue 6.2jt. CAC = 2.4jt / 80 = Rp 30k. ROAS = 6.2 / 2.4 ≈ 2.6×. Campaign contribution = 6.2jt × 62% − 2.4jt ≈ Rp 1.44jt. If those 80 guests return four times at Rp 28k and 62% margin, CLV ≈ Rp 69k vs CAC 30k — healthy on paper. Attribution is messy; last-click lies.
