Landscape

How Indonesians pay

E-wallets

GoPay, OVO, Dana, ShopeePay — often the customer’s first tap. The merchant may see them as QRIS.

QRIS

One merchant QR, many apps. The interoperability story of the last few years.

Virtual account

Bank transfer with a unique code. Common for invoices and checkout.

Cards

Visa / Mastercard / JCB / local. Growing, still not the default at a warung.

BNPL

Kredivo, Akulaku, SPayLater and peers. The merchant may be paid sooner than the customer pays.

COD

Cash on delivery. Still used in e-commerce. High operational and fraud cost.

Dropped the old ‘70% of digital / 30% BNPL / 20–30% COD’ cluster — unverified mix for 2026. Qualitative only.

QRIS · the rule

Standard for QR — not a duty for every stall

What is true

  • QRIS is Bank Indonesia’s national QR standard
  • Payment service providers that offer QR payments must use QRIS
  • A merchant registers with one licensed PSP and can accept many apps

What is overstated

  • “QRIS is mandatory for all merchants” — no: a cash-only warung is not breaking a QR law by staying cash
  • “QRIS is always free” — MDR exists; some bands are 0%

Bank Indonesia QRIS pages: PSPs offering QR must use the standard. Merchant adoption is policy-supported, not a universal merchant mandate.

Read the left column twice. This is the correction the brief demanded.

Controls

Payment is where cash walks out

Payout account

QRIS and marketplace must settle to the business bank, not a cousin’s Dana.

Daily match

POS tickets vs PSP report vs bank. Ten minutes. The leak is in the unmatched.

Refunds

Who can void a QRIS sale? Dual control if you can; a log if you cannot.

Same access sermon as Session 01, now with money movement.

BNPL and COD

When the customer is not the payer yet

BNPL

A licensed party often pays the merchant (minus a fee) and collects later. Your counterpart is the provider’s settlement, not the shopper’s salary date. Read the contract: principal vs agent again.

COD

Revenue when delivered and accepted — and cash when the courier remits. Failed delivery and fake receipts are inventory and fraud problems.

Do not deep-dive product names. Risk picture only.

Quick check

Which sentence is accurate?

A

Every Indonesian merchant is legally required to accept QRIS.

B

If a PSP offers QR payments, those QR codes must follow QRIS.

C

MDR is always 0% for everyone after October 2026.

D

Net settlement is the same number as sales revenue.

Correct: B. C is false — 0% is banded. D is the whole problem of this class.
