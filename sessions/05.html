Four levels

From ‘what happened’ to ‘do this’

Descriptive

What happened?

Sales by day, channel, SKU. The close.

Diagnostic

Why?

Tuesday died because rain + no weekday promo.

Predictive

What next?

Ramadan demand, stock-out risk, churn.

Prescriptive

Do what?

Reorder, recut the ad set, call the slow payer.

Most UMKM and most student projects should master descriptive + diagnostic. Pretend-ML is not a grade boost.
Students love ‘AI forecast’. Pull them back to a clean daily sales file.

Reconcile or it is fiction

Three ties every week

01

Platform ↔ bank

Settlement file vs credits. Missing days are missing revenue or missing fees.

02

Ads manager ↔ GL

Served spend vs prepaid vs expense. Dashboards lie in timezone and attribution.

03

POS ↔ tax

Omzet vs e-Faktur / PPh basis. Coretax will not invent your missing notes.

This is the accountant’s BI. If they remember one slide, this is it.

Fraud and cyber

Analytics as a control

Signals worth a look

  • Refunds clustered on one staff PIN
  • Ad spend spikes at 02:00
  • Bank payout to a new personal account
  • GMV up, bank down

Access

Who can export, who can withdraw, who can change the pixel. Analytics without access control is a leak with charts.

Cohorts, simply

When they arrived matters

A cohort is everyone who first bought in the same week or month. Ask: are January buyers still here in March? If not, your CAC was a rental, not an asset.

Café version

Guests who first came during a campus promo. How many returned without a second voucher?

Seller version

Buyers from a live-sale week. Return rate and refund rate vs organic buyers.

No need for a cohort matrix on screen. The idea is enough. Then a sample dashboard layout.

Studio

Six tiles for Warung Sari

15 minutes · pairs

Design a one-screen weekly dashboard. For each tile: name, source system, formula, and the GL line it must match (or “no GL — qualitative”).

  • At least two finance tiles and two digital tiles
  • One fraud / exception tile
  • One tile you refuse to show the owner (vanity) — and why
Hear two pairs. Ban ‘number of likes’ as a core tile.
