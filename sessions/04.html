Content system

Pillars beat inspiration

Kopi Nusantara · 4 pillars

  • Education — beans, brew, water
  • Behind the bar — people, roast, mess
  • Customer voices — UGC, reviews
  • Offers — weekday, not every post

80 / 20 habit

Most posts help or entertain. A minority sell. A feed of only discount codes trains people to wait — and trains your P&L to live on margin-destroying promos.

Ask them to name three pillars for the laundry / bika they used last week. Kill ‘we post when we remember’.

Paid social · Meta

Campaign → ad set → ad

Campaign

The objective

Awareness, traffic, or conversions. Do not mix them in one soup.

Ad set

Who, how much, where

Audience, daily cap, schedule, placement. This is where money leaks.

Ad

Creative + promise

Image or video, copy, CTA. Test 3–5, not one ‘perfect’ piece.

custom audience lookalike daily cap
Targeting: demo, interest, website visitors, lookalike of buyers. Beginners: cap the day, wait several days, watch ROAS and contribution — not likes.

Paid social · TikTok

Native or it dies

Formats you will meet

  • Spark Ads — boost a real organic post
  • In-feed — native video in For You
  • Shop / live — demand and checkout together

Budget hygiene

Minimums and auction prices move. Treat any ‘minimum Rp per day’ you read in a blog as dated until you open Ads Manager. Creative that looks like a TV spot usually loses to a phone video with captions.

Old decks quoted a hard Rp 200,000/day TikTok minimum. We will not. Open the live account, then lock a cap you can afford to lose as tuition.
We removed the unverified daily minimum. Same spirit as Google Ads last session.

Success is not views. Success is incremental weekday tickets you can see in the POS, minus the pastry you gave away, minus the fee. If you cannot name the POS report, you cannot claim ROAS.
Teaching budget. Ask how they would tag the POS (a simple ‘heard from IG’ tally is enough for a café).

WhatsApp is the shop floor

Treat the inbox as a ledger of promises

Catalogue

Prices match the counter and the tax-inclusive decision. A wrong price in WA is a credit note waiting to happen.

Labels

New / regular / complaint. That is CRM for a café. Export is a control problem.

Broadcast

Consent and an exit. A blast to a scraped class group is not a strategy — it is a reputation hit.

One minute. Then reputation.

Reputation

A bad review is an operations ticket

Do

  • Reply in public, fix in private
  • Offer a credit you can book
  • Log who is allowed to speak as the brand

Do not

  • Buy fake reviews
  • Argue calories with a customer in comments
  • Delete a complaint you have not fixed
Refunds and make-goods need the same account every time. Social is where the expense is born.
Connect to Session 07 credit notes. Fake reviews = ethics + documentation.
