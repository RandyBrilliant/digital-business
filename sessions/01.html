01 · Definition

What is digital business?

Any organisation that uses digital technologies as core infrastructure to create, deliver, and capture value — not as a brochure on the side.

It is

  • Internet and platforms as the operating system
  • Data used to decide, not only to decorate a report
  • Processes that can run without a person retyping them
  • Reach that is not limited by one street

It is not

  • A Facebook page bolted onto an analog shop
  • Buying software and never changing the process
  • Moving paper PDFs into a shared drive
  • A cost centre IT “handles later”
Have a student try a definition. Kill ‘we have Wi-Fi so we are digital’. Create / deliver / capture is the strategy sentence they will reuse in Session 08.

01 · Accounting view

The same firm, a different ledger

Revenue

When is it earned?

Marketplace payouts, vouchers, delivery fees, gift cards. PSAK 72 will haunt Sessions 02 and 08.

Cost

What is the real take-rate?

Ads, MDR, commission, free shipping you actually fund, returns.

Control

Who can change the number?

Admin rights on Shopee, Jurnal, and the bank app are an internal-control topic.

PSAK 72 pembukuan pengendalian internal MDR
This is why the course sits in an accounting programme. Digital business without controls is just a faster way to lose cash.

02 · Framework

Four levers of transformation

People

Who works how

Owner, barista, admin, accountant. New roles: community, ads, reconciliation.

Process

Order to cash

From “write it in a book” to order → pick → pay → settle → recognise.

Technology

The stack

WhatsApp, marketplace, QRIS, cloud books (Jurnal, Accurate), POS.

Data

Evidence

Sales by SKU, unpaid settlements, ad spend, tax invoices in Coretax.

If you change only technology, you have bought software. Transformation is when the other three levers move with it.
Draw a 2×2 if the room is sleepy. Order-to-cash is the process they already know from intro accounting. Coretax is DJP’s core tax system — e-Faktur now lives there, not as a forever-separate app.

02 · Where finance sits

Cloud accounting is not optional décor

Order-to-cash

Marketplace or QRIS sale → settlement file → bank → revenue and fees. Someone must match all four.

Procure-to-pay

Supplier on WhatsApp → PO or chat trail → goods in → e-Faktur masukan → payment. Missing any step and PPN credit dies.

Jurnal Accurate e-Faktur Coretax DJP rekonsiliasi
Name Jurnal and Accurate as the local cloud-accounting pair students will meet at internships. Do not demo logins — classroom Wi-Fi. Stress matching settlement files.

03 · Comparison

Traditional vs digital operations

TraditionalDigital
MarketingBrochures, radio, outdoorTargeted ads, content, search
SalesOne counter, one cityMarketplace, social commerce, D2C site
PaymentCash, transfer, maybe EDQRIS, e-wallet, VA, BNPL, cards
RecordsBuku kas, delayed closePOS + cloud books, daily close
CustomerWalk-in, memoryCRM, reviews, broadcast lists
ScaleLimited by floor spaceLimited by operations and cash
Last row is the trap: digital scale is not infinite. Warehouses, returns, and working capital still exist. Accountants get paid to say that out loud.

04 · Ecosystem

The stack around an Indonesian firm

Demand

Search, social, marketplaces, WhatsApp. Shopee, Tokopedia (TikTok-controlled), TikTok Shop, Instagram.

Fulfilment

JNE, SiCepat, GoSend, in-house ojek. Late delivery is a refund and a review.

Money

QRIS, GoPay, OVO, Dana, ShopeePay, VA, Midtrans / Xendit.

Books

Jurnal, Accurate, spreadsheet plus bank. The spreadsheet is a system until it is not.

Tax

Coretax DJP, e-Faktur, e-Bupot, PPN and PPh on marketplace activity.

Trust

Reviews, ODOL-free claims, original-goods badges — and the audit trail behind them.

Tokopedia: TikTok completed a deal for 75.01% on 31 Jan 2024; GoTo kept 24.99% (Reuters / GoTo disclosure). Say it once; Session 02 repeats it.

Facts we will keep honest

Claim you may have heardWhat we will say instead

This slide is the honesty contract for the course. If a number cannot be sourced, we drop it. 13% SME stat is retired. Mitra ‘30 million’ is retired — last widely published company figure was far lower and dated.

05 · Cases

Three shapes of “gone digital”

Platform native

Shopee / TikTok Shop seller

Demand lives on someone else’s app. Your P&L is GMV minus take-rate, ads, and returns.

Hybrid UMKM

Warung + QRIS + WA

Still a physical room. Digital is payment, catalogue, and a broadcast list. Books often lag.

Digital product

Course, SaaS, voucher

No warehouse. Revenue recognition and refunds become the hard problem.

Kopi Kenangan and Erigo are useful stories of scale. We will not recite unverified revenue or valuation figures in this room.
Old deck quoted Erigo Rp 300B and Kopi Kenangan as a $1B unicorn without sources we trust for 2026. Directional stories only.

Worked example

Warung Sari, Helvetia

Fictional neighbourhood shop. Cash tin last year. This year: QRIS, WhatsApp catalogue, one Shopee listing for parcels.

What changed

  • Sales no longer equal cash in the tin
  • QRIS settles T+1 / T+2, minus MDR
  • Shopee pays net of commission and ads
  • The owner still buys stock in cash at the pasar

What the student must build

  • A daily sales journal that is not “cash received”
  • A receivable for unsettled QRIS and Shopee
  • Fee accounts, not netted silently into sales
  • A closing habit: bank + platform + buku
Teaching fiction. Helvetia is a real Medan district so it feels local. This example returns in Sessions 06 and 09.

Risks you will meet

Cyber, fraud, and fat-finger admin

Access

One owner password on Shopee, bank, and Gmail. A staffer leaves and still has OTP.

Fraud

Fake buyer, ATO on the ads account, supplier who never ships. Digital fraud is still fraud.

Tax

Marketplace withholding does not mean the seller’s PPh/PPN file is done.

Internal control for a two-person shop: separate the person who posts the listing from the person who can withdraw to a personal account, if you can. If you cannot, review the bank every day.
Keep it concrete. OTP sharing is the case they recognise. Preview Session 05 fraud analytics without promising software.

Quick check

Which statement is true?

A

A business is digital once it has a website, even if orders are only taken on paper.

B

Digital business uses technology as core infrastructure to create, deliver, and capture value.

C

Marketplace GMV is always the seller’s revenue under PSAK 72.

D

QRIS is legally mandatory for every Indonesian merchant, including a cash-only warung.

Correct: B. C is false (principal vs agent). D overstates the rule — QRIS is the national standard for QR payments, not a duty for every stall to accept QR.

Hands up. Then reveal B. Park C for Session 02 and D for Session 06 so they stay hungry.
