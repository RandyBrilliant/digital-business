Why

The opportunity is real. The 13% is not our number.

What we can say

  • UMKM are most of Indonesian firms and a large share of jobs and GDP
  • QRIS merchants are now in the tens of millions, mostly UMKM (BI, mid-2026)
  • Onboarding to a platform is not the same as running books or earning margin

What we will not say

  • “Only 13% of SMEs are digital” — stale, method unclear
  • A single official digital-penetration % for 2026 — sources disagree
  • Mitra Bukalapak “30 million+” — not a figure we can stand behind

BI QRIS merchant figures: Session 06. UMKM contribution to GDP / employment: long-running government talking points (~60% GDP, vast majority of employment) — treat as order-of-magnitude, not this week’s BPS print.

The gap is depth: payments yes, pembukuan and tax no. That is the professional opportunity for this class.

Roadmap · phase 2

Digital payment · month 2–3

To-do

  • Payout to the business account
  • Simple POS or at least a daily sheet

Not true

  • “QRIS is always free” — MDR bands exist
  • Accepting QRIS means your books are done

Roadmap · phase 3

Digital marketing · month 3–6

Calendar

3–4 honest posts a week. Owner named.

Small paid

A cap you can afford to lose as tuition. Measure tickets, not vibes.

Broadcast

WhatsApp to people who opted in. Google posts for local SEO.

Old slides said Rp 500k–2jt per month. Treat that as a planning band for a serious micro brand, not a rule. Many stalls should spend less until contribution is known.

Reuse Session 03–04. Do not let them jump here before payments and a sheet exist.

Roadmap · phase 4

Digital operations · month 6+ (or earlier if messy)

Books

Cloud accounting — Jurnal or Accurate are the names they will meet — or a disciplined sheet. Stock, AR from QRIS and platforms, fee accounts.

Tax

Coretax DJP is the core administration system. e-Faktur is the tax-invoice module many PKP now use inside it. e-Bupot for withholding. A marketplace payout is not a faktur.

Jurnal Accurate e-Faktur Coretax e-Bupot
This is the accounting destination of the whole course. SIAPIK can be mentioned as BI’s simple bookkeeping push if you want a public-sector option.

Cases · carefully

Stories we can teach without fake precision

Erigo

Bandung fashion brand that used Instagram and e-commerce to scale. Useful as a channel story. We will not quote an unverified ‘Rp 300B revenue’.

Kopi Kenangan

App-ordering kiosk brand that scaled stores nationally. Useful as data + loyalty. We will not treat a unicorn headline as a 2026 audited fact in this room.

Mitra-style warung programmes

Platforms that digitise kiosks (inventory, pulsa, bills). Historically large. Live partner counts change — do not recite “30 million.”

Maxim vs incumbents

Price-sensitive ride-hailing. Useful as a ‘different take-rate’ story, not a full strategy case unless you research it.

Honesty over spectacle. Students can research current figures for the capstone with sources.

Programme noise

Free is a word with fees

Actually free

Google Business, WA Business, many bank QRIS onboards, SIAPIK-style simple books.

Looks free

Marketplace listings that pull you into ads and funded shipping.

Never free

Your time, returns, MDR bands, and the accountant you eventually need.

Government UMKM digital programmes exist and rename themselves. Do not list a 2022 grant as current unless you checked.

Order-to-cash for an UMKM

The loop you are really installing

01 Order

WA, counter, or listing.

02 Fulfil

Hand over or pack. Stock down.

03 Collect

Cash / QRIS / platform. Receivable if not cash.

04 Recognise

Sales, fees, tax artefacts. Then bank match.

If any step has no owner, the roadmap will stall at the sticker on the fridge.
